## General Style Notes (apply to all 5 images)

- **Character:** Use the character that Gemini has built to represent me — keep the same character consistent across all 5 images
- **Style:** Sketchnote / visual explainer, hand-drawn marker pen aesthetic (same style as @pvergadia / thecloudgirl.dev)
- **Background:** Clean white
- **Text:** Bold, slightly hand-lettered feel
- **Colours:** Bright primaries — red, blue, green, yellow; pastel fills for zones/boxes
- **Arrows:** Large, sweeping, directional — mix of straight and curved
- **Decorations:** Lightning bolts ⚡, stars ✨, question marks ?, checkmarks ✓ as accents
- **No photorealism** — illustrative and playful throughout
- **Aspect ratio:** 16:9 landscape for all images (Medium header format)
